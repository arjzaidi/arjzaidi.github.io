About this course

This course introduces the concepts, data sources and geospatial tools used to map hazards and assess risk. Students work with international disaster databases, climate and disaster reports, and GIS-based approaches to understand how hazards become disasters and how risk can be planned for and managed.

Lecture materials are provided as text-based web pages with reading lists, assignments and self-check quizzes. Original slide files (PDF and PPTX) are available for download from each lecture page.
