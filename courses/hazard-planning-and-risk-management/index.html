About this course

This course enables students to critically understand hazard planning and disaster management processes, their challenges and achievements, in the context of the Pakistan disaster management system. It explores the relationship that makes people vulnerable and increases the frequency of natural hazards and corresponding disasters.

Topics include hazard mapping, risk and vulnerability analysis, and evacuation planning using GIS and spatial analytical software to address different hazard and disaster management phases.

Lecture materials are provided as text-based web pages with reading lists, assignments and self-check quizzes. Original slide files (PDF and PPTX) are available for download from each lecture page.
